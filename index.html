Mobile App · Education

StudyBuddy AI

A mobile learning companion built exclusively for college students. Students pick a course from the library — spanning programs like Computer Science, Business, Midwifery, and more — then ask the AI questions about their lessons or assignments and get instant, course-specific support, with chat history saved by topic. The goal was to make studying feel less like searching through scattered notes and more like having a tutor who already knows the course material on hand. Every response is grounded in the student's actual curriculum, so answers stay relevant instead of generic, and the whole experience was designed to be quick to open, easy to navigate, and genuinely useful during a late-night cram session.
